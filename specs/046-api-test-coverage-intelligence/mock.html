        Definitions

        Requirement states
        • Not covered: no scenario targets it.
        • Generated, not executed: mapped, no attributable result (never run, not in the selected run, or blocked).
        • Executed, failed: a check relevant to this requirement was evaluated and failed.
        • Verified: a relevant check was evaluated and passed on an unedited request.
        • Inconclusive: result exists but proves nothing (timeout, check not evaluated, edited request, no relevant check).
        • Stale: evidence exists but the requirement's contract changed after the run. Always shows the reason and needs re-execution; never shown as not executed. Reserved; not produced yet.
        • Precedence: not covered, generated, then failed over verified over inconclusive.
        Denominators
        • Operations: eligible (selected) operations.
        • Parameters, request schemas, response schemas, response codes: eligible requirements of that kind; ranges and default count as their own response keys.
        • Assertions: evaluated assertions, excluding not-evaluated, edited-request and no-response results.
        • Categories: eligible requirements of that category.
        Priority factors
        • Executed failure, requirements without a scenario, no scenarios at all, generated-not-executed, inconclusive.
        • Destructive method, write method, declared security requirement, contract complexity.
        • Failure history: not available, so not used.
